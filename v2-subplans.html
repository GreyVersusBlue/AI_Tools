Plans for the parts of the job that aren’t teaching.

Eight single-file web apps for rosters, seating, schedules, grade math, and paperwork. No install. No account. Nothing uploaded. Open the file, use it, close the tab. Once it has loaded it keeps working with the wifi off.

01PERIOD Name Picker 5 seconds. Pick a name, move on. random / cold-call / groups Nobody gets called twice until everybody has been called once. Open 02PERIOD Seating Chart Generator Build it once, reshuffle whenever you need to. seating / roster / shuffle Lock the seats you already fought for. It deals around them. Open 03PERIOD Schedule Browser Look up any teacher, room, or period in seconds. schedule / rooms / periods Useful during coverage. Extremely useful during a fire drill. Open 04PERIOD School Layout Visualizer See the whole building’s schedule, hyperlinked. building / map / clusters Turns a spreadsheet nobody reads into something you can point at. Open 05PERIOD Final Grade Checker Double-check the math before report cards go out. grades / report-cards / math Catches the rounding argument before the email about the rounding argument. Open 06PERIOD Image → PDF Assembler Turn a stack of photos into one clean PDF. pdf / images / scans Assembled in the tab. Saved to your machine. Uploaded nowhere. Open 07PERIOD Prompt Builder Answer a few questions, get a ready-to-use AI prompt. prompt / ai / template For when a colleague asks how you got it to do that. Open 08PERIOD Word Doc Merger Combine multiple Word docs into one, in order. word / docx / merge Assembled in the tab. Uploaded nowhere. Open
