Behavior & Points TrackerIdea A quick per-student tally or point system you can run live during class.
Exit Ticket / Bell Ringer GeneratorIdea Printable half-sheets or a rotating bank of daily warm-up prompts.
Rubric BuilderIdea Build, save, and print grading rubrics.
Field Trip Permission Slip GeneratorIdea Fill in the trip details once, get a printable permission slip.

Digital Hall Pass / Sign-Out LogIdea Track and print who’s out of the room and when.
