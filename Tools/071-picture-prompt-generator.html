Picture-Prompt Speaking/Writing Task Generator

Use the starter pictures or upload your own, and get a random prompt for a speaking or writing task in the target language.

Scenes that come with the tool: a market, a kitchen, a park, a classroom, a bus stop and more. There are no words in them, so they work for any language, and they are never copied into your storage.

Task prompts

Starter sets are written in the target language, so the projected and printed task wording matches the class. Switching sets changes which prompts are used everywhere. Edit any of them — your changes stay in that set.

Upload at least one image, or include the starter pictures, to get started.
