Grading rules
Overall grade Each bucket's score is the total points earned ÷ total points possible in that bucket, then the two are blended at these percentages. The two always add to 100.
Missing work Scores entered as M, Z, MI, MIS or INC always count as missing. EX, EXC and dropped (*) assignments are left out.
