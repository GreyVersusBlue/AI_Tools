    Good to know before you merge
    • Text, headings, bullet/numbered lists, tables, and images are carried over from every file.
    • The page size, margins, and orientation of the last file in your list are used for the whole merged document.
    • If different files use very different custom styles or fonts, some formatting may fall back to a default look rather than matching the original exactly.
    • Headers/footers from individual files aren't merged in — only the content between them.
    • Corrupted or non-.docx files are skipped automatically and listed after merging, so one bad file won't stop the rest.
