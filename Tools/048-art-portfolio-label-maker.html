Student Art Portfolio Label & QR Tag Maker

Labels for physical art portfolios — each one can carry a photo thumbnail and a QR code holding the artist statement, no server or upload needed.

Portfolio entries

Example: Self-Portrait — Ava R.  Charcoal on paper, inspired by...

Add a photo to a row to print a thumbnail on the label. The QR code encodes the artist statement text directly (readable offline by any QR scanner) — it doesn't link anywhere, since nothing here is hosted online.
Print layout
The reference sheet is one compact list of every piece — title, artist, and the full statement — for your own records and for gallery signage. No photos or codes on it, so it stays short and cheap to print.
