Questions sent to you

    Lets you keep more than one game board saved at once (e.g. one per unit).

    Splits a saved roster into teams by count instead of typing team names by hand. Rosters saved elsewhere on this device under “np_rosters” show up in the dropdown below. Team names are pre-filled and stay editable on the scoreboard afterward, same as typed-in teams.

    Each question can carry a picture — a map, a diagram, a political cartoon, a primary source. Use + Image on a row. Pictures are shrunk on the way in, kept in this browser’s image storage (and carried inside a JSON export), shown with the clue on the projector, and printed on the practice quiz and answer key.

    Spreadsheet needs four columns, one row per question: Category, Points, Question, Answer. The first row should be that header row.

    Every team answers each clue. When the answer is shown, mark each team right, wrong or no answer, then press Score this clue. A right answer scores the clue’s points; a wrong answer or no answer scores nothing. A Daily Double is still one team’s wager. While the marks are showing: 1-9 change that team’s mark · Enter scores the clue.

    One last question, after the board or whenever you start it. Each team writes down a wager: a whole number from 0 up to its score, and a team at 0 or below may wager up to 100. You type the wagers in, and they stay hidden until all are in. Then the question is shown, then the answer, and you mark each team right or wrong. A right answer adds the wager; a wrong answer takes it off. While the marks are showing: 1-9 change that team’s mark · Enter scores the round.

    Toss-up questions are open to every team. You record the buzz: press the team that buzzed first (or its number), then Right or Wrong. A wrong answer locks that team out of the question and costs what is set below (nothing, unless you change it). A right answer scores the toss-up and earns that team alone a bonus question. Questions come from the source in its order, none twice in a round, and the points go on the scoreboard. Keys while a toss-up is open: 1-9 the team that buzzed · R right · W wrong · Esc close.

    A spin chooses the clue, not a team. The wheel has one wedge for every clue not yet played, and a spin is as likely to land on one wedge as on any other. Press Spin the wheel (or the S key): the result is written under the button, and the chosen clue takes the focus on the board, so Enter opens it. A clue can still be opened by hand. The spins of a game are worked out from a number kept with the board, so reloading the page does not change the next spin; Reset game starts a new sequence.       Share the bank

      Save the whole bank as a file for a colleague or another computer, and bring one in. The bank file keeps everything, each question’s picture included; the spreadsheet is for reading and editing in Excel or Sheets. A file you choose is shown first: what it would add, what it would change and what it would leave out, and nothing is stored until you press Add. Importing adds to this bank and never deletes from it: a question already here is updated or skipped, so the same file can be imported twice.
