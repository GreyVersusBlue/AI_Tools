Historical Figure / Country Trading Card Maker

Batch-build trading cards for a research project — photo/flag and stats on the front, facts on the back.

Print and PDF lay out fronts, then backs (row-mirrored) — flip the whole printed stack along its long edge before reprinting for the backs.

Decks

Each deck keeps its own cards, theme, and set name — e.g. one per class period or unit.

Share by link or QR code (card text, stats, themes, and rarity — not photos, which stay on this device) or download the whole deck, photos included, as a file. To send finished cards with photos, use Download PDF or All PNGs (zip) above.

Review game

Play this deck as a class review game on the projector. Two cards come up face down, the team on the clock sees theirs and answers “what do you remember about this figure?”, then picks a stat to challenge the next team with. Higher value takes the point. You run it — no timers, no student devices.
