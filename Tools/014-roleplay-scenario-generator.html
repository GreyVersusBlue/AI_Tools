What a good attempt sounds like

One short line per criterion. It prints as a strip on every handout and role card, and shows on the projector — so a pair can check their own attempt against it before they call you over. Saved per class, like the scaffolding fill-ins, since what counts as good in Spanish 1 isn’t what counts in Spanish 3.
